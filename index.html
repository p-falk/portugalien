🏔️ Quiz Climber

A climb from mountain to moon — one correct answer at a time.

📋 How it works

The climb is split into levels, each with a few questions. Pick the right answer to move up; every level has its own picture and theme.

⚠️ One rule

A wrong answer sends you back to the start of the level — so think before you click! Your score keeps what you earned along the way.
